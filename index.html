How to use

Drag any node away from or towards the center.

Away means positive change; towards means negative change.

Connected nodes move after a short delay. Positive links carry the same direction of movement; negative links reverse it. The whole string flashes as each signal passes.

Disturbances continue propagating until you choose Return to rest.

Each connection represents an observed association, not a cause.
